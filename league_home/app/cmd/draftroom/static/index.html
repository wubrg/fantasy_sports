Positional bias

How much of every edge is about the position, not the player. Subtracted in the vs Pos column.

Second projection

ECR is FantasyPros' expert-consensus positional rank. FP is their projection re-solved into dollars against the same pool as Value, so the two are a like-for-like second opinion — where they diverge is what a single number would hide. QB interceptions are estimated (FantasyPros omits them), so QB FP is modelled, not published.

Price lines

What a bid buys at each position. The muted figure is what this league has paid for that finish in past drafts. It says what the money buys, not how a player will do — price rank and finish rank correlate about +0.46 here, and top-five money landed a top-five finish 28 times in 60.

Scarcity

PS% is the share of value left at a position after its best remaining player goes. Startable counts the players above the position's replacement bar — the same players only above replacement leaves on the board. N yours is that count over your own board, once your one-per-offense / no-handcuff preferences take the offenses you already own a piece of off it; blocked players are flagged and dimmed in the table.

Traits — what kind of player he is
floor Points from repeatable usage — catches and yardage, not touchdowns. Rarely wins or loses you a week.
redzone Points concentrated in scoring plays. Cornering touchdown equity — boom or bust.
upside The median understates him: the industry sees upside, or he is projected past his own record.
targets Raw projected target volume — the stickiest thing a pass-catcher has.
bellcow A back carrying both the rushing and the passing-down work, not a committee share.
discounted Price suppressed by an injury tag. You buy the discount and the risk together.
